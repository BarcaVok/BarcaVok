Jaký je vliv prostředí a demografie na politické zastoupení žen? **Cílem** projektu je zjistit, zda existují společné sociodemografické a socioekonomické faktory, které charakterizují volební okrsky, ve kterých získávají ženy preferenční hlasy. ## This repository showcases examples of my work in SQL, Python and Power BI

### 1. The project—"Where Do Women get preferential votes?"—is my final project for the Digital Academy—data from Czechitas—and was created in collaboration with Anna Musílek.

What is the impact of environment and demographics on women’s political representation? **The goal** of the project is to determine whether there are common sociodemographic and socioeconomic factors that characterize the electoral districts in which women receive preferential votes. As part of the project, we analyze the results of the 2025 elections to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. We are working with data from the Czech Statistical Office (election results and the 2021 Census of Population, Dwellings, and Buildings), data from PAQ Research, data from the Register of Territorial Identification, Addresses, and Real Estate, the National Register of Healthcare Providers, the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports, and the Register of Economic Entities. **Tools used:** Keboola, SQL, Snowflake, VS Code, Python (Pandas, Jupyter, Geopandas, Folium), PowerBI, AI.
